ION Connectivity Monitor

The Monitor watches every ION at every site of this tenant, data centers included, while a page of it is open: an ION going offline or coming back is logged here and pops up at the bottom right. It checks every 20 s; turn on Watch IONs while you need a close eye: every 5 s, with an optional sound, also with this tab in the background, until you turn it off or close the tab. Times are the controller's own. The log is kept by the Monitor, so every browser and tab sees the same one, and it survives a restart. A change while nothing was watching (Monitor closed) is logged when watching resumes, marked while away.

An ION that loses power is often shown as online by the controller until it reconnects, minutes later. The Monitor still catches it when it comes back: it is logged as reconnected at the controller's reconnect time, with its reboot, but there is no pop-up when it goes offline.

Every event is still logged; only matching ones pop up, play the sound and are listed below. The filter lasts until this tab is closed.

For IONs not at a site, including new ones not claimed yet: a new ION is logged the first time it connects to the controller. These always pop up (the filter doesn't apply). Kept by the Monitor for this tenant; press Enter or click away to save.
